Chat with ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude — all at once, side by side.

Parallel Chat opens all three leading AI assistants in a single browser tab. Type your question once, hit Send All, and watch the answers appear simultaneously. No more copy-pasting. No more tab-switching. Just instant, side-by-side comparison.


WHY USE MULTIPLE AI MODELS?

No single AI is best at everything — and they often disagree in ways that matter.

Each model has different strengths.
ChatGPT excels at creative writing and coding. Gemini is connected to real-time web information and Google's ecosystem. Claude is known for nuanced reasoning and handling long, complex documents. Using all three means you always have the right tool — without having to guess which one to open.

Cross-validate for accuracy.
When all three models agree, you can trust the answer. When they disagree, you know the topic deserves a closer look. Relying on a single model means you'll never notice when it's confidently wrong.

Eliminate blind spots.
Every AI has gaps in its knowledge and reasoning. What one model misses, another often catches. Seeing all three responses at once surfaces the blind spots you'd never discover with a single AI.

Reduce bias.
Each model was trained on different data using different methods. Comparing responses side by side makes it easier to spot when an answer reflects a built-in bias rather than a reliable fact.

Get the best answer faster.
Parallel Chat sends your message to all three AIs simultaneously and lays out the answers in one view — so you can read, compare, and decide in seconds.


KEY FEATURES

- Send All: One message delivered to ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude at the same time
- Side-by-side panels: Three resizable panels in a single window; drag the dividers to resize
- Session history: Conversations are auto-saved; resume any previous session with one click
- File attachments: Attach images and documents to send to all AIs at once
- Panel toggle & reorder: Show or hide any AI panel, and drag the top toggle buttons to rearrange the panel order
- Dark / Light theme: Switch all panels between dark and light mode at once — the theme is synced into each AI's embedded view
- Keyboard shortcut: Open Parallel Chat instantly with Ctrl+Shift+M (Cmd+Shift+M on Mac)
- Language toggle: Switch the UI between English and Korean at any time


HOW IT WORKS

Parallel Chat embeds each AI service in its own panel inside a single Chrome extension page. Your existing login sessions are preserved — sign in to each service once and they stay logged in across sessions.

No new accounts, no API keys, no subscriptions required. Works entirely with your existing ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude accounts.


KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Claude panel — previous chat list: Because of how Claude's website behaves inside an embedded frame, the sidebar that normally lists your previous conversations is not shown inside the Claude panel. Your conversations are still saved to your Claude account as usual — to browse past chats, open claude.ai/chats in a regular tab. A small in-app notice points this out the first time you open the Claude panel.


PRIVACY

Parallel Chat does not send user data to the developer’s own servers. The extension stores only limited local data in the user’s browser, such as UI preferences, panel layout, language/theme settings, and session references needed to restore previously opened chats.

When the user chooses to send a prompt, message content and any attached files are transmitted directly to the selected third-party AI services inside their respective panels, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude, in order to provide the extension’s core functionality. These transmissions are governed by each provider’s own terms and privacy policies.

Parallel Chat does not sell user data, does not use user data for advertising, and does not transfer user data to third parties except as necessary to provide the requested functionality through the user-selected AI services.
